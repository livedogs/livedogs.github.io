Privacy Policy

We use cookies on this website to give you the best possible experience, allowing us to improve our site for our users. There is no reason to be concerned about the privacy implications of the cookies we use. Any information gathered is completely anonymous and no private details are stored. However, we do recognize the importance of making you aware of them. What is a cookie? A cookie is a small text file, which asks permission to be placed on your browser or hard drive. The file is used to collate web traffic data, and in no way gives us access to your computer or any information about you, other than the data you choose to share with us. How can I manage the use of cookies? You can choose to accept or decline cookies at any time, however, most browsers are set up to typically accept cookies automatically. If you continue without changing your settings, we’ll assume that you are happy to receive all cookies on the https://www.livedogs.ca/ website. However, if you would like to, you can block our cookies at any time. This can be done by activating the ‘block cookies’ setting on your browser, allowing you to refuse the placing of cookies. However, please note that if you do use your browser settings to block all cookies, you may not be able to access all or parts of our site. The functionality and viewing experience may also be impaired.
